**Selection, horizons and weighting.** The draw count is from `data/processed/makerdao_eth_a_draw_events_analysis.csv`. Candidate clean repaid spells are reconstructed from the separately tracked lifecycle file. Filtering nonpositive duration removes 136 of 7,947; restricting starts to 2020 or later removes another 706; there is no subsequent published-FX endpoint loss for either currency. The selected sample is much smaller and opens earlier than the full draw sample. The same-selected-start 12-month medians of 0.56% ARS and -4.70% TRY versus 2.67% and 6.59% for all draw starts hold horizon and FX source constant. Therefore the observed-versus-fixed comparison combines horizon, historical path, selection and FX-frequency differences. An urn is a position, not a verified person. 6,221 urns have one draw; 2,713 have more than ten and account for 70.29% of all draw events. Amount-weighted fixed-12m net outcomes are 12.64% ARS and 27.95% TRY, but those pooled ratios are dominated by large draws and timing. Pooling after urn aggregation leaves the pooled weighted ratio unchanged by algebra; the first-draw-only estimate uses fewer events.

**Additional scenario bounds.** `results/prior_quote_sensitivity.csv` applies the strictly earlier published *calendar date* at each clean-spell endpoint, rather than allowing same-day retrospective reference marks. It does not establish an executable quote. `results/gas_conversion_sensitivity.csv` varies round-trip gas across USD 0, 20, 40 and 80 and each-way conversion friction across 0%, 0.3% and 1% for both observed spells and all-draw 12-month scenarios. `results/illustrative_ars_premium_sensitivity.csv` varies the *ratio* of accessible-rate wedges at exit versus entry across 0.80, 1.00, 1.10, 1.25 and 1.50. At an assumed 1.10 ratio, the selected ARS median is +6.21%; at 0.80 it is -27.82%. Neither value is an estimate of the actual ARS parallel-market path. The component CSV verifies for each pooled sample that gross FX less interest, fee, conversion and gas equals weighted net to numerical tolerance; a median is not the sum of component medians.

**Conditional identities checked by tests.** The voluntary route pays accrued debt, an exit conversion friction and voluntary closing gas and gets collateral back. The liquidation route instead uses marked collateral and any returned residual; issuer claim impairment is accrued debt minus collateral recovery, and penalty can be collected only from the surplus. For each completed sale, recovered debt plus impaired debt equals accrued debt, and recovered debt plus penalty plus borrower residual equals sale proceeds. No conditional liquidation route repays debt again at the planned horizon.

**Cost timing.** The round 20% rate is an illustrative effective annual scenario, not a measured LCU financing cost; it compounds as (1+r)^t and is bracketed by 5%, 10%, 40%, 80% and 120% in the daily observed-duration table. A 0.5% fee on the original USD-equivalent principal is paid externally at origination, not financed. Conversion frictions apply on entering a USD position and, only upon voluntary closing, on reacquiring LCU for debt. USD 40 gas is a USD 20 opening and USD 20 voluntary closing budget, not an observed gas price for every event.

**Corrected claim.** Previously published -4.44% and -0.47% scenario-weighted risk-adjusted medians are invalid as expected liquidation outcomes, were not retained in the manuscript and are not generated by the current code. The terminal screen is a hypothetical month-12 ratio comparison in the Appendix, not path-dependent liquidation or an asset-specific ETH/BTC model. Official ARS FX observations have no validated executable parallel counterpart in this package; the ARS findings are explicitly restricted to an official-rate counterfactual. The daily ECB cross-rate is informational and neither daily series establishes an intraday transaction rate.

**Reproduce.** Run `bash run_all.sh` from the repository root. Its cash-flow and daily-date checks, rebuilt CSVs, fourteen generated results tables, figures, manuscript PDF, earlier response PDF and one-page model note should agree. The manuscript's sample-construction and cost-assumption tables use the tracked selection file and stated model inputs.
