**Selection and weighting.** The draw count is from data/processed/makerdao_eth_a_draw_events_analysis.csv. An urn is a position, not a verified person. 6,221 urns have one draw; 2,713 have more than ten and account for 70.29% of all draw events. Amount-weighted fixed-12m net outcomes are 12.64% ARS and 27.95% TRY, but those pooled ratios are dominated by large draws and timing. Pooling after urn aggregation leaves the pooled weighted ratio unchanged by algebra; the first-draw-only estimate uses fewer events.

**Conditional identities checked by tests.** The voluntary route pays accrued debt, an exit conversion friction and voluntary closing gas and gets collateral back. The liquidation route instead uses marked collateral and any returned residual; issuer claim impairment is accrued debt minus collateral recovery, and penalty can be collected only from the surplus. For each completed sale, recovered debt plus impaired debt equals accrued debt, and recovered debt plus penalty plus borrower residual equals sale proceeds. No conditional liquidation route repays debt again at the planned horizon.

**Cost timing.** The rate is effective annual and compounds as (1+r)^t; a 0.5% fee on the original USD-equivalent principal is paid externally at origination, not financed. Conversion frictions apply on entering a USD position and, only upon voluntary closing, on reacquiring LCU for debt. USD 40 gas is a USD 20 opening and USD 20 voluntary closing budget, not an observed gas price for every event.

**Corrected claim.** Previously published -4.44% and -0.47% scenario-weighted risk-adjusted medians are invalid as expected liquidation outcomes, were not retained in the manuscript and are not generated by the current code. The terminal screen is a hypothetical month-12 ratio comparison, not path-dependent liquidation or an asset-specific ETH/BTC model. Official ARS FX observations have no validated executable parallel counterpart in this package; the ARS findings are explicitly restricted to an official-rate counterfactual.

**Reproduce.** Run bash run_all.sh from the repository root. Its cash-flow checks, rebuilt CSVs, six generated results tables, figures, manuscript PDF, response PDF and one-page model note should agree. The manuscript's sample-construction and cost-assumption tables use the tracked selection file and stated model inputs. The processed MakerDAO inputs and original official FX CSVs are tracked; the large raw archive has a documented checksum and pointer.
